## Campaign description
RF Safe (Quanta X Technology LLC) answers inbound calls to its customer line with an AI assistant. During a call, a caller can ask to have a link texted to them, such as a product page or an article. Texts are one-to-one replies to that caller's request, sent only to the number they are calling from. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.

## Message flow / call to action
Consumers opt in verbally during an inbound phone call they place to RF Safe at +1 727-677-5572. The call begins with a disclosure that it is answered by an AI assistant and may be recorded. When the caller asks for a link, the assistant asks: "Is the number you're calling from, ending in XXXX, okay to text?" Texts are sent only after the caller says yes, and only to the number the caller is calling from: the assistant cannot text any other number, and callers with blocked caller ID are not texted. The opt-in is logged with the time and number. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Terms: https://callbots.org/terms. Privacy: https://callbots.org/privacy. Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

## Keywords and replies
- Opt-in (confirmation after verbal yes is not sent separately; START re-subscribes): START, UNSTOP, YES -> "You are resubscribed to RF Safe. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."
- Opt-out: STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, REVOKE -> "You are unsubscribed from RF Safe texts. No more messages will be sent. Reply START to resubscribe."
- Help: HELP, INFO -> "RF Safe support (AI assistant). Visit https://quantacase.com, email support@quantaxtechnology.com or call this number. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out."
