What is SAR? SAR, specific absorption rate, is how fast the body absorbs radio-frequency energy from a phone, in watts per kilogram. The US FCC limit is 1.6 watts per kilogram over 1 gram of tissue, and 0.08 watts per kilogram whole-body. RF Safe shows six FCC-reported SAR values for each phone: head, body, and hotspot, each with cellular only and with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth on at the same time. Use sar_lookup to give a caller their phone's numbers.

Are FCC limits safe? RF Safe's position is no. The 1996 limits are based only on short-term heating in a 220-pound adult male mannequin. Biology runs on timing and bioelectric signaling, not just heat. Melnick and Moskowitz, 2026, in Environmental Health, applied standard EPA risk modeling to the NTP cancer data and found the 0.08 watts per kilogram whole-body limit is 15 to 900 times too high for cancer risk, and 8 to 24 times too high for male reproductive safety.

What did the big animal studies find? The 30 million dollar US National Toxicology Program study, 2018, found clear evidence of malignant heart schwannomas and some evidence of brain gliomas in male rats. The Ramazzini Institute study, Falcioni 2018, found the same heart schwannomas at exposures near the public limit. A 2025 WHO-commissioned systematic review, Mepham and colleagues, rated the evidence for both tumor types as high certainty.

Is there proof of non-thermal effects? Yes. The FDA-approved TheraBionic P1 device treats inoperable liver cancer with amplitude-modulated radio frequency at levels 100 to 1,000 times below phone SAR limits, with no heating, acting through Cav1.2 calcium channels.

Why do some studies find nothing? RF Safe treats null results as expected boundaries: carrier frequency, pulsing, polarization, tissue type, genotype, and timing all change the outcome. Never say every study shows harm, and never say RF alone is proven to cause a specific human disease.

What is low-fidelity biology or S4-Mito-Spin? These are RF Safe's interpretive frameworks, so call them hypotheses. Low-fidelity biology: pulsed, polarized wireless signals add timing noise to the cell's bioelectric signaling. S4-Mito-Spin has three pillars: voltage-sensor timing in ion channels, mitochondrial and NOX oxidative stress, and radical-pair spin chemistry. Density gating explains why heart Schwann cells, brain glia, and testes are hit first.

Children? Studies such as Gandhi 2012 and Christ 2010 found children's heads can absorb up to about twice the localized brain SAR of the adult mannequin. RF Safe has kid-versus-adult SAR visuals for each phone that can be texted once texting is on.
