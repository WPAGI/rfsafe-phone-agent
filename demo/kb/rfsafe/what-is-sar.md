SAR (Specific Absorption Rate) is the rate at which the body absorbs radio-frequency energy from a device, measured in watts per kilogram. In the United States the FCC limit for phones is 1.6 W/kg averaged over 1 gram of tissue.

RF Safe publishes SAR comparisons for phones so people can see the reported values side by side.
