Best Deal On Bot is not in the Chrome Web Store yet. During early access, Quanta X Technology staff send the extension folder to approved businesses. Ask the Quanta X Technology staff member you work with to request access, or request early access at bestdealon.app/app.

To install in Chrome: unzip the file you received, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder, then pin Best Deal On Bot. Create a free Best Deal On account, create an API token on your dashboard, and paste it into Best Deal On Bot under Business, Sync with bestdealon. Keep the folder where it is; to update, replace the folder and click reload.

Upgrading from Bizinator Backup: replace the files in the existing folder and click Reload; don't remove it, so your data is kept.

New business pages stay private until you click "I own this business" and an admin approves them. Best Deal On may verify a business phone number with an automated call and a one-time code.

Callbots phone assistant onboarding: a business signs up, turns on calls (texting is coming soon), and fills an empty knowledge base with Grok Bot's help.
