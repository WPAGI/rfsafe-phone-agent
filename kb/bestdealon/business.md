Best Deal On (bestdealon.app) publishes local business profiles written by the businesses themselves: hours, specials, FAQs and contact details that people, search engines and AI assistants can trust. Each profile has a web page, a machine-readable JSON profile and an RSS feed. Best Deal On is by Quanta X Technology LLC.

Best Deal On Bot is a Chrome browser extension that works with Grok Bot. It keeps a business's Best Deal On page up to date, gives one source of truth for hours, specials, FAQs and links, and backs up the business's Grok Bot bots. The page updates when you sync.

Bizinator is a zero-person-company business harness from Quanta X Technology. It is built on the Callbots AI agent stack, so a business can be run by AI agents. Bizinator and Promptinator are Best Deal On products. The phone assistant is powered by Callbots.org.
