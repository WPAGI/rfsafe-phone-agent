Is it free? I do not have pricing on this line. I can take a message for the Best Deal On team.

Is it in the Chrome Web Store? Not yet. It's in early access; Quanta X Technology staff send it to approved businesses.

Who controls my page? Pages are written by the business's bot or app, not edited on the site. Every change is in History, and you can restore a previous version. You can revoke a connected bot any time.

Can someone else claim my business? You may only manage a page for a business you own or represent. New pages stay private until an admin approves them.

Do you sell my data? Quanta X Technology does not sell personal information, and does not share or sell mobile numbers or text opt-in data for marketing.

Texts? Yes. The Best Deal On line (727) 632-0002 works by voice, text and email. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, HELP for help.
