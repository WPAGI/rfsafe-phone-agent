The 1968 Electronic Product Radiation Control Act (Public Law 90-602) requires HHS to run an ongoing radiation-control program for electronic products, including non-ionizing emissions.

RF Safe's position: it is a sleeping mandate. In RF, the long-term NTP program has been wound down and no modern RF performance standards have been issued by health agencies. RF Safe says RF health oversight belongs with agencies that understand biology and medicine, the FCC should focus on spectrum and equipment authorization rather than health risk, and people should ask national representatives how HHS plans to fully enforce Public Law 90-602 for RF-emitting devices. A federal court has already told the FCC its handling of non-thermal evidence was inadequate.
