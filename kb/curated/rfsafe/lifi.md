Li-Fi is wireless networking that carries data on light (visible or infrared) instead of radio waves. The IEEE 802.11bb standard covers light-based networking. Source: rfsafe.org/class/start and the rfsafe.org IEEE 802.11bb archive.

RF Safe's position (its "Light-Age" vision): use radio only where mobility is essential, and carry most indoor data over light (Li-Fi) and wired Ethernet. Phones and laptops connect by light or wire when they are stationary, and bedrooms, classrooms, clinics and offices are designed to be low-RF by default. RF Safe says moving schools to wired and light-based networking is one of the fastest ways to cut chronic exposure without giving up bandwidth. This is RF Safe's advocacy position, not a regulatory finding.

Everyday step RF Safe suggests: prefer wired connections and, where available, Li-Fi or Ethernet indoors for heavy data use. More: https://rfsafe.org/mel/story.php?slug=the-clean-ether-light-age-roadmap and the forum topic https://rfsafe.org/forum/topics/optical/
