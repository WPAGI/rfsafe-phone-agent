RF Safe's position: many "anti-radiation" products have red-flag designs and "99% blocking" slogans look flimsy. TruthCase is meant to obey RF physics and train safer habits.

What makes it different: a conductive flap that sits between you and the phone during calls and pocket carry; an ultra-thin build near antenna zones so the phone does not ramp up power; no metal strap loops, magnet/plate sandwiches, large ear-side openings or thick wallet stacks.

TruthScore is RF Safe's public five-point red-flag checklist for any case: metal near antenna regions, detachable magnet/plate constructions, large unshielded ear-side openings, thick multi-slot wallets in front of radios, and "99% blocking" claims without whole-device tests. TruthCase is built to score 0 out of 5. Disclosure: RF Safe sells these products.
