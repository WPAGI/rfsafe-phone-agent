SAR (Specific Absorption Rate) is the rate at which the body absorbs RF energy from a phone, measured at the head and body. RF Safe's position: SAR is not the whole story, but it is a useful tool when treated honestly; use it alongside S4-Mito-Spin, not instead of it (rfsafe.org/class/start).

Where to look: a single phone's SAR page is https://rfsafe.org/mel/SAR.php?phone=<model-slug> (for example apple-iphone-17-pro-max). Compare two phones side by side at https://www.rfsafe.org/tools/sar-compare/. The kid-vs-adult view is https://www.rfsafe.org/SAR-share/sar.kids.php?slug=<model-slug>&region=head (or body). For a specific model, use the SAR lookup tool to read the exact figures.
