Section 704 of the 1996 Telecom Act prevents local governments from denying cell-tower placements solely on the basis of the "environmental effects" of RF emissions if FCC limits are met.

RF Safe's position: Section 704 is one of two legal choke points. In practice school boards and city councils cannot fully weigh health concerns even when residents demand it, and 1990s heat-only limits get treated as untouchable even as non-thermal evidence grows. RF Safe asks people to ask local officials and school boards why Section 704 still gags them on health concerns.
