S4-Mito-Spin is RF Safe's unifying mechanism for non-thermal effects of weak RF and ELF fields.

S4: voltage-gated ion channels (especially calcium and sodium) have charged S4 segments that act as voltage sensors. RF Safe proposes that polarized, pulsed fields force ions near the membrane to oscillate and nudge S4 at the wrong times, so calcium signaling loses timing fidelity. Most affected are timing-critical tissues: heart, neurons, Leydig and germ cells, activated immune cells.

Mito: mitochondria and NADPH oxidases (NOX) turn noisy calcium timing into excess reactive oxygen species (ROS), creating tissue-specific oxidative stress over months and years.

Spin: red blood cells have neither S4 channels nor mitochondria, yet show rouleaux (stacking) after short phone exposures. RF Safe's bridge is spin-dependent radical-pair chemistry in heme and flavin cofactors (hemoglobin, NOX, cryptochrome).

This is RF Safe's proposed framework, not an established regulatory finding.
