The QuantaCase contact page is https://quantacase.com/pages/contact. It has a contact form for help choosing a case, using a QuantaCase, or checking an order: tell them your phone model and how they can help, and include your order number for order questions. The page also lists (727) 677-5572 to call or text the automated support assistant for questions about QuantaCase, phone radiation, SAR information, or RF research, with helpful links sent to your phone or email.
