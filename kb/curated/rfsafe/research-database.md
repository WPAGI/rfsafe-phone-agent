RF Safe's research database at rfsafe.app/research holds 6,477 original source records of studies on radio-frequency and electromagnetic field effects, of which 6,165 are structured records. Each study is labeled by effect, evidence level and study type, and the whole list can be searched and filtered by effect, evidence, study type and year, or browsed as plain pages.

The study summaries on the site are AI-assisted summaries of third-party papers; the original papers are linked for each record. Offer to text the rfsafe.app/research link.
